# Natural Ocean

A sea of its own for Kerbal Space Program 1: waves raised by the weather's wind, white water where they break, surf,
seaweed, craft that ride the waves, and a camera that can go half under. A first sea, not a finished one. It needs
[Keystone](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-keystone). The short version is on the repository's front page.

* [The long description](The-long-description.md): everything the front page said before it was cut short, and what was tried on each system
* [The plan](Plan.md): what is made and how, what is wrong with it, what comes next, and how it fared on Windows;
  kept true as the work goes
* [The research](Research.md): what real seas do (waves, breaking, foam, surf, the light in water) and how seas are
  drawn in real time
