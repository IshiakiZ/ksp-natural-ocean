# Natural Ocean

A sea of its own for every world that has one: waves raised by the weather's wind, swell, white water where they
break, surf on the beach, seaweed in the shallows, rain on the water, the splash of a crash, and a camera that can go
half under.

**A first sea, not a finished one.** It reads as a sea at a glance; it does not yet stand beside a photograph of one.

![The sea in ten pictures, from a light air to a gale, the surf, under water, towards a low sun](src/NaturalOcean/img/ocean-tour.jpg)

For Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x. **Needs [Keystone](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-keystone) 0.8 or later**, a separate download
([on SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone)): this mod does not load without it. With
[Natural Weather](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-natural-weather), which is not needed, the sea goes by the weather's wind; without it,
by a light breeze.

## What it does

* **Waves from the wind,** higher and longer the harder it blows, with swell from elsewhere.
* **White water** where crests break, and foam that lies and fades.
* **The shore:** surf that breaks and runs up the sand, green water over the shallows, seaweed on the bottom.
* **What floats rides the waves.**
* **A crash makes a splash as big as the blow:** a crown that tears into strands, a jet, mist, and white water in rings.
* **Rain on the sea:** rings where drops strike, a matt grey sea under overcast.
* **From high up,** one broad sheen where the sun is mirrored, and patches of gusts and slicks.
* **A camera half under:** the waterline across the glass where the sea cuts it, and the water dimmer and bluer with
  depth.

## Install

Copy the `NaturalOcean` folder from the download into your KSP's `GameData`, beside `Keystone`.

## Settings

In the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Alt-K; Option-K on a Mac): how rough, how
clear the water is, whether craft ride the waves, seaweed, and whether other seas are put out of sight.

## Good to know

* **Still wrong:** fresh foam is too clean and what floats leaves no wake. [The plan](docs/Plan.md)
  says what comes next.
* The game's and Scatterer's seas are put out of sight where this one is drawn (Scatterer's settings are not
  touched). Beside other mods that draw the sea (Parallax, EVE) it has not been tried.

## Works on

Mac: tried. Windows 11 (Direct3D 11 and OpenGL): 0.1 tried; this version built for it, not run there. Linux: never run.
